# StageArt Blueprint Addendum
# Organization Information Screen — Confirmed Addition

This document records the additional confirmed requirement for the Organization Information screen.

## 団体ロゴ

The Organization Information screen must provide an Organization logo image upload operation.

- **団体ロゴ** — image upload is supported.
- Only **1 image** can be registered as the Organization logo.
- The registered logo can be replaced by uploading another image.
- The logo update is included in the Organization Information screen's **更新** operation.
- After the update is saved, the Organization public page reflects the updated logo.

This is an addition to Section 15.4 団体情報 of `docs/12-FunctionalStructure.md` and is a confirmed business/UX requirement.
